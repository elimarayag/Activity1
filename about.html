    01

    WHO I AM

    I like turning ideas into things people can see, use, and experience.

    I'm a multidisciplinary creative who brings ideas to life through technology and visual storytelling. I work across web development, mobile applications, interface design, and visual art—combining technical thinking with a strong creative perspective.

    My goal isn't simply to make something that works. I want every project to feel intentional—from the way information is organized to the smallest visual detail a user interacts with.

    WHAT I DO

    DEVELOPMENT

    Digital experiences built to work.

    I develop responsive websites and applications with attention to usability, structure, performance, and the experience people have while using them.

    UI / UX DESIGN

    Interfaces with purpose.

    VISUAL ART

    Ideas beyond the screen.

    &

    TWO SIDES · ONE PRACTICE

    Somewhere between logic and imagination.

    Development taught me how to think in systems. Art taught me how to communicate without always needing words.

    I bring both into my work. I care about whether something functions correctly, but I care just as much about how it feels to look at and interact with.

    DEVELOPMENT DESIGN ART CREATIVITY
    03

    THE ARTIST SIDE

    Creativity isn't an extra.

    It's part of how I think.

    My background as a visual artist has shaped the way I approach technology. Years of exploring illustration, painting, and digital media taught me to pay attention to composition, contrast, emotion, and storytelling.

    That perspective follows me into development and design. Even when I'm writing code, I'm thinking about what the person on the other side of the screen will see and feel.
