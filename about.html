    • About Me

    Hi! I'm a multidisciplinary creative who brings ideas to life through technology and visual storytelling. I specialize in websites and mobile applications, with a passion for building clean, functional, and user-friendly digital experiences. From front-end interfaces to interactive prototypes, I craft products that are not only beautiful but also meaningful and intuitive.

    I have experience developing responsive websites and cross-platform mobile applications using modern frameworks and tools. Whether it's a sleek landing page, a fully functional e-commerce site, or a mobile app with seamless navigation, I ensure every project is built with performance, accessibility, and user experience in mind.

    Beyond development, I have a strong eye for design. I can design user interfaces from scratch—focusing on usability, hierarchy, and visual harmony. I enjoy working with color, layout, and typography to create UI that’s not just usable, but delightful. My design process always starts with empathy: understanding the user, the problem, and how to solve it with thoughtful, elegant solutions.

    What sets me apart is my background as a visual artist. Creativity is the thread that runs through everything I do. I’ve spent years exploring various forms of art—illustration, painting, digital media—which gives me a unique perspective when approaching design and development. My artistic background allows me to bring a strong sense of aesthetic and originality to every project I take on.
